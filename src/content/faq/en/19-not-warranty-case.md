question: "What is not covered by the warranty?"
---

The warranty does not cover faults resulting from:

- mechanical damage;
- improper use;
- unauthorised repair or modification of the lock;
- damage caused by water or other liquids, where the model is not designed for such conditions;
- damage after an impact, break-in or attempted forced entry;
- use of unsuitable batteries or rechargeable batteries;
- normal wear and tear of consumable parts.
