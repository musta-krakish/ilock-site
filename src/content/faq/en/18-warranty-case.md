question: "What is considered a warranty case?"
---

A warranty case is a lock malfunction caused by a manufacturing defect or faulty components, provided the operating instructions have been followed.

For example:

- the lock no longer recognises a fingerprint despite a working power supply;
- the electronic part of the lock does not work;
- the display, reader or another electronic component is faulty;
- the lock does not respond to its standard opening methods despite a working power supply;
- a mechanism malfunction has occurred due to a manufacturing defect.
